DESCRIPTION
Pewter Stoa is a personal wellness learning system that teaches yoga, breathing, mindfulness, meditation, relaxation, flexibility, and steady daily routines - privately, on your device. After you install the app, it keeps working with Airplane Mode on. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. It is not just a meditation timer. You get a colonnade of practice you can walk without a subscription or a signal: personalized sessions, breathing, sleep, and a private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud. Stoa is an on-device teacher that picks a session from your aspect (ease, sleep, stretch, or sit), how many minutes you have, how the grain feels, the hour, and what you already practiced. You get a daily lesson, a streak, and minutes this week. Timed yoga and mobility sequences come with teaching cues: Morning walk, Plinth unknot, Hip range, Spine stoa, Dusk shade, Hamstring column. Breath walks use four rising columns: Four columns, Stoa 4–7–8, Long range, Tablet pulse. Night practices for bed include Body shade, Count the bays, Worry on the plinth, and Legs on the plinth. Pose bays offer short lessons covering setup, breath, and if a joint objects. Tablet is a private journal — fully offline. Notes stay on this iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. Choose day mist or night shade, or match the phone. Open Walk. Read the lesson of the hour. Walk the session the teacher chose, or open Range and pick your own. Each step has a title, a cue, and a timer. Hold or skip. When the last step ends, the session is filed in a private log on this iPhone. Breathing is four columns that rise and settle — not a remote coach video. Sleep practices are written for a dark room. If you fall asleep during a body shade, that is the practice. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Teaching copy ships inside the app. Your tablet notes, preferences, and practice log live in local storage. There is no camera and no tracking in this product. Good for anyone who wants yoga, breath, and rest without a feed, a login, or a cloud.
