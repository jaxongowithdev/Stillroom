DESCRIPTION
Lichen Kiln is a personal wellness learning system that teaches yoga, breathing, mindfulness, meditation, relaxation, flexibility, and steady daily routines - privately, on your device. After you install the app, it keeps working with Airplane Mode on. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. It is not just a meditation timer. You get a kiln of practice you can sit with without a subscription or a signal: personalized sessions, breathing, sleep, and a private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud. Kiln is an on-device teacher that picks a session from your glaze (ease, sleep, stretch, or sit), how many minutes you have, how the bisque feels, the hour, and what you already practiced. You get a daily lesson, a batch streak, and minutes this week. Timed yoga and mobility sequences come with teaching cues: Morning bisque, Shelf unknot, Hip glaze, Spine kiln, Dusk ash, Hamstring shelf. Breath vents use two rising bars: Four vents, Kiln 4–7–8, Long glaze, Folio pulse. Night practices for bed include Body ash, Count the heat, Worry in the flue, and Legs on the shelf. Pose shelf offers short lessons covering setup, breath, and if this. Folio is a private journal — fully offline. Notes stay on this iPhone. Swipe to delete. Nothing is uploaded. Choose sand bisque or night umber appearance, or match the phone. Open Hearth. Read the lesson of the hour. Fire the session the teacher chose, or open Batch and pick your own. Each step has a title, a cue, and a timer. Hold or skip. When the last step ends, the session is filed in a private log on this iPhone. Breathing is two kiln vents that rise and fall — not a remote coach video. Sleep practices are written for a dark room. If you fall asleep during a body ash, that is the practice. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Teaching copy ships inside the app. Your folio pages, preferences, and practice log live in local storage. There is no camera and no tracking in this product. Good for anyone who wants yoga, breath, and rest without a feed, a login, or a cloud.
