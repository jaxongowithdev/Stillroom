DESCRIPTION
Gesso Attic is a personal wellness learning system that teaches yoga, breathing, mindfulness, meditation, relaxation, flexibility, and steady daily routines - privately, on your device. After you install the app, it keeps working with Airplane Mode on. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. It is not just a meditation timer. You get an attic of practice you can prime without a subscription or a signal: personalized sessions, breathing, sleep, and a private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud. Attic is an on-device teacher that picks a session from your wash (ease, sleep, stretch, or sit), how many minutes you have, how the paper feels, the hour, and what you already practiced. You get a daily lesson, a streak, and minutes this week. Timed yoga and mobility sequences come with teaching cues: Morning prime, Attic unknot, Hip canvas, Spine attic, Dusk wash, Hamstring frame. Breath frames use a growing picture frame: Four frames, Attic 4–7–8, Long wash, Sketch pulse. Night practices for bed include Body wash, Count the frames, Worry in the primer, and Legs on the easel. Pose frames offer short lessons covering setup, breath, and if this. Sketch is a private journal — fully offline. Notes stay on this iPhone. Swipe to delete. Nothing is uploaded. Choose gesso canvas or night attic appearance, or match the phone. Open Easel. Read the lesson of the hour. Prime the session the teacher chose, or open Canvas and pick your own. Each step has a title, a cue, and a timer. Hold or skip. When the last step ends, the session is filed in a private log on this iPhone. Breathing is a picture frame that grows and shrinks — not a remote coach video. Sleep practices are written for a dark room. If you fall asleep during a body wash, that is the practice. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Teaching copy ships inside the app. Your sketch pages, preferences, and practice log live in local storage. There is no camera and no tracking in this product. Good for anyone who wants yoga, breath, and rest without a feed, a login, or a cloud.
