DESCRIPTION
Brume Lamp is a personal wellness learning system that teaches yoga, breathing, mindfulness, meditation, relaxation, flexibility, and steady daily routines - privately, on your device. After you install the app, it keeps working with Airplane Mode on. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. It is not just a meditation timer. You get a lamp of practice you can dim without a subscription or a signal: personalized sessions, breathing, sleep, and a private journal — fully offline. No account. No cloud. Lamp is an on-device teacher that picks a session from your aim (unwind, sleep, stretch, or sit), how many minutes you have, how the body feels, the hour, and what you already practiced. You get a daily lesson, a wick streak, and minutes this week. Timed yoga and mobility sequences come with teaching cues: Dawn wick, Window unknot, Hip cairn, Spine glass, Dusk ember, Hamstring lamp. Fog glass breathing uses a filling bar: Glass of four, Ember 4–7–8, Fog out, Wick pulse. Night practices for bed include Body lantern, Ceiling fog, Worry on the sill, and Legs on the sill. Pose chimney offers short lessons covering setup, breath, and if this. Pages is a private journal — fully offline. Notes stay on this iPhone. Swipe to delete. Nothing is uploaded. Choose dark lamp or light glass appearance, or match the phone. Open Lamp. Read the lesson of the hour. Light the session the teacher chose, or open Lessons and pick your own. Each step has a title, a cue, and a circular wick timer. Hold or skip. When the last step ends, the session is filed in a private log on this iPhone. Breathing is a glass that fills and empties — not a remote coach video. Sleep practices are written for a dark room. If you fall asleep during a body lantern, that is the practice. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no remote AI. Teaching copy ships inside the app. Your pages, preferences, and practice log live in local storage. There is no camera and no tracking in this product. Good for anyone who wants yoga, breath, and rest without a feed, a login, or a cloud.
